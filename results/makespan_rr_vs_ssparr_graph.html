Key Experimental Findings

  • Cluster Makespan Conservation: In non-idling batch task execution, makespan is governed by the bottleneck VM, preserving total datacenter wall-clock completion.
  • Architectural Separation: Validates the two-tier cloud design: global brokers balance datacenter makespan while local schedulers optimize responsiveness.
