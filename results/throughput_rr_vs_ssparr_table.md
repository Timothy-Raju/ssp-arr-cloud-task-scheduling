## Analysis & Takeaways

1. **System Throughput Stability**: System throughput (tasks completed per second) is bounded by the bottleneck VM's capacity under non-idling execution.
2. **Equal Total Completion Capability**: SSP-ARR delivers dramatic latency and responsiveness benefits while maintaining identical cluster-wide job throughput.
