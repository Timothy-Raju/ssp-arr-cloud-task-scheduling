## Analysis & Takeaways

1. **Zero Energy Overhead**: Host power consumption is driven by CPU utilization and active makespan under the SPECpower linear profile. SSP-ARR executes tasks without increasing idle power or host runtime.
2. **Energy Proportionality**: Energy consumption scales gracefully from ~0.31 kWh at 100 tasks to ~2.32 kWh at 2000 tasks across all 30 datacenter hosts.
