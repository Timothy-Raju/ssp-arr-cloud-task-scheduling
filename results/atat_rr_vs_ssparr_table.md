## Analysis & Takeaways

1. **Scaling Turnaround & Completion Gains**: Turnaround and completion time improvements scale from +5.1% up to +46.6% as workload contention grows.
2. **Elimination of Convoy Effect**: By preempting long jobs in volatile burst phases and prioritizing shorter tasks with adaptive quantum slicing, SSP-ARR eliminates queue convoys and saves millions of cumulative seconds in Total Completion Time (TCT).
